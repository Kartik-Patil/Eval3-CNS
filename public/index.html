Captcha + OTP + SSL — Demo

A simple demonstration of server-side CAPTCHA validation, email OTP, and local HTTPS. This is a demo — do not use in production as-is.

How it works

Request an OTP after solving the captcha. The server stores the captcha in your session, and the OTP is emailed to your address.
